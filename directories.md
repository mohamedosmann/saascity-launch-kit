# The list

150 launch platforms, SaaS directories and startup listing sites — the free, curated subset of the SaaSCity dataset.

**Domain Rating** is Ahrefs DR, refreshed 2026-07-31. **Link** is what an approved listing actually gives you.
Pricing is the entry tier, not the ceiling — most freemium sites sell a featured slot on top.

> Working down this list by hand takes a weekend. That is the intended use.
> The full 2,404-directory dataset (with submission URLs, review times and dead-link sweeps) is at [saascity.io](https://saascity.io).

## Where the data comes from

- **Domain Rating** — Ahrefs, via their free DR API. Refreshed weekly.
- **Link type** — checked by hand on a live listing, not taken from the site's own marketing copy.
- **Base list** — [launchdirectories.com](https://launchdirectories.com) plus SaaSCity's own crawl of 2,404 directories, deduped by domain.

Found a dead one, or a DR that has moved? [Open a PR](CONTRIBUTING.md) — that is what this repo is for.
